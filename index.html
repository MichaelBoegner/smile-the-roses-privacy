Smile the Roses

Privacy Policy · Last updated October 11, 2026

Smile the Roses is a small app that grows roses while you smile. It was built to be private by design: there are no accounts, no ads, and your face never leaves your phone.

The camera

The app uses your front camera only to notice whether you are smiling. Each camera frame is analyzed on your device and then immediately discarded. The app never shows, records, saves, or uploads any photos or video of you.

What is stored on your phone

Your garden (the roses you have grown) and the progress of an unfinished session are saved on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This data stays on your phone and is deleted when you delete the app.

Anonymous diagnostics from Google ML Kit

Smile detection is powered by Google's ML Kit, which runs on your device. By default, ML Kit sends Google anonymous diagnostic and usage information so Google can keep the library working well: device model and operating system version, the app's version, performance measurements, error codes, and a per-installation identifier that is not intended to identify you. It does not include any images, video, or anything about your face. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure for details.

What we don't do

We don't collect your name, email, location, contacts, or any other personal information. We don't use analytics or tracking of our own, we don't show ads, and we don't sell or share data with anyone.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes
